S3 · Who we are

Who We Are

Equipping Boots
On The Ground

From the very beginning, HEF believed that God wanted us to raise up, support and encourage the local Haitian body of Christ to minister to the community.

God has continued to bless HEF, ensuring that our ministry has never stopped. As more families move toward the Central Plateau where we serve — leading to increased school enrollment and a greater need for food, space, teachers, and logistics.

We have been able to sustain and expand our work by equipping and empowering over 350 Haitians to serve and teach within their own communities. Thanks to our partnership with Maranatha Church, HEF has successfully grown its ministry efforts year after year.

More than 95 cents of every dollar goes straight to Haiti, funding the work that is going on there right now.

S5 · Happening now

On The Ground

Happening Right Now

Alongside the programs that run every day, this is what your giving is building at the moment.

20 New Classrooms

Twenty new village classrooms are under construction across the plateau.

Land For Expansion

We purchased land adjoining one of our churches to make room for what comes next.

Care For The Displaced

Meals and essential care for over 450 people who have had to leave their homes.

Youth Camp

More than 200 young people come together for a week of camp each year.

VBS In 8 Villages

Summer Vacation Bible School reaches over 1,000 children across eight villages.

Women’s Learning Center

Equipping women with valuable, income-earning skills.

Cargo Shipments

Containers of medications, Bibles, sound equipment and school supplies, arriving on an ongoing basis.

Wells & Solar Water

A new well and a solar-powered distribution system, most recently in a village on the plateau.
